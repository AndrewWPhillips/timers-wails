# Timers – Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2026

Go Timers ("the app") is published by the Silver Gopher. This policy explains what information the app handles and what happens to it.

## Summary

Go Timers does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information. The app has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no telemetry, and it does not connect to the internet. Everything it stores stays on your device, or in a folder that you choose yourself.

## Information stored on your device

To work, the app saves the following in a settings file in your user profile (under your Windows application data folder):

- the timers you create (labels, durations, colours and their current state)
- your presets and alarm settings, including the location of any sound file you pick as the alarm
- the size and position of the app window
- if you turn on syncing: the folder you chose, a random device identifier generated by the app, and a device name (by default your computer's name, which you can change)

This information never leaves your device unless you turn on syncing, as described below. Uninstalling the app removes it.

## Syncing timers between devices (optional)

If you choose a sync folder, the app writes your timer changes, its random device identifier and the device name to files in that folder, so that your other devices running Go Timers can read them. The app does not send these files anywhere itself.

If the folder you choose is synchronised by another service (for example OneDrive, Dropbox or a network share), that service will copy the files according to its own terms and privacy policy. Turning off syncing stops the app writing to the folder. You can delete the files in it at any time.

## Third-party components

The app displays its interface using Microsoft Edge WebView2, which is part of Windows. Microsoft may collect diagnostic data from WebView2 according to your Windows privacy settings and the Microsoft Privacy Statement. The Microsoft Store may also collect information about your installation and purchase according to Microsoft's policies. Go Timers itself receives none of this information.

## Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published with a new effective date alongside the app's next update.
